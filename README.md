## Projects

**[India's economy as a working model](https://github.com/jiteshkprasad/indian-economy)**
One connected map of India's whole economy: every rupee, who pays it and who receives it. 128 accounts and 3,893 money
flows for 2023-24, built from 32 official sources, where every account adds up to the rupee and every number says how
it was made. Try it at [indianeconomy.jitesh.app](https://indianeconomy.jitesh.app).
`Python` `Next.js` `Cloudflare Workers`

**[Solmere](https://github.com/jiteshkprasad/solmere)**
A harbour town of 50 people run by AI for 30 days: jobs, money, gossip, a daily newspaper and an election. What they
say, choose and vote comes from AI. Watch season 1 at [solmere.jitesh.app](https://solmere.jitesh.app).
`TypeScript` `Three.js` `LLM agents`
